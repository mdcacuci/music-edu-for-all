Events

Upcoming concerts, drives, and community programs

A Magical Journey from West to East: Music from Disney and Studio Ghibli

Date: August 16 | Location: Southern Nevada Music

Music Education for All presents a fundraising concert celebrating the music, storytelling, and cultural influence of Disney and Studio Ghibli. The program features familiar melodies and distinctive musical traditions in a concert experience celebrating the universal language of music. Proceeds support our mission to expand access to meaningful music education and create opportunities for students to learn, explore, and connect through music.

Music Education at YMCA Summer Camp

Date: June 15–18 & July 28–30 | Location: YMCA of Southern Nevada - Durango Hills Community Center

Music Education for All provides hands-on music education experiences for 1st and 2nd grade students through engaging, accessible instruction and exploration. Students are introduced to fundamentals of music with interactive activities and exposure to violins, violas, guitars, pianos, and percussion instruments. The program emphasizes accessibility, curiosity, and active participation to help young students discover the joy of music.
